SwimSwim

SwimSwim shows your swim workouts from Apple Health, builds training plans around your target pace, and analyzes your swimming technique from photos or videos you choose — all on your iPhone.

Common questions

  • No swims are shown. Allow Health access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → SwimSwim, and make sure your swim workouts are in Apple Health.
  • Technique analysis can't open my video. Allow photo access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → SwimSwim.
  • The Import button in a training plan is unavailable. Sending a plan to the Workout app requires iOS 18 or later and a paired Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later.
  • Do I need an account or subscription? No. SwimSwim has no account and all features are free.
