Privacy Policy

SwimSwim · Last updated: November 6, 2026

SwimSwim ("the App") is a swimming app that shows your swim workouts from Apple Health, builds training plans around your target pace, and analyzes your swimming technique from videos you choose. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what data the App uses and how it is handled.

Summary

  • The App does not require an account.
  • Health data and videos are processed on your device only.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not upload your health data, photos, or videos to our servers.

Information We Collect

SwimSwim does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to us. The App does not include analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking in its current version.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, the App reads swimming-related data from Apple Health, including swimming workouts, swimming distance, stroke count, heart rate, and active energy. This data is used only to display your swim records, statistics, and trends inside the App. If you allow it, the App may also save swimming workouts to Apple Health.

  • Health data is processed locally on your iPhone.
  • We do not send Health data to our servers or to any third party.
  • We do not use Health data for advertising, marketing, or profiling.
  • Health data is never stored in iCloud by the App.

Photo Library and Videos

The App requests access to your photo library so you can choose swim photos and videos for technique analysis. Only the items you select are used.

  • Body pose detection and technique scoring happen locally on your device using Apple's on-device frameworks.
  • Selected videos and analysis results are stored only within the App on your device.
  • We do not upload your photos or videos to our servers.

Data Storage

Your target pace, app settings, and technique analysis reports remain on your device. The App does not sync your activity to a cloud account. Deleting the App removes this data from your device. Data saved to Apple Health is managed by Apple Health and can be removed in the Health app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. The App only checks with Apple whether you have an active subscription. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

The current version of SwimSwim does not integrate third-party analytics, login providers, or social networks. If this changes in a future update, this policy will be updated before release.

Children's Privacy

SwimSwim is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Your Choices

You can change or revoke Health access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health, and photo access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos. Without Health access, swim records will not be shown; without photo access, technique analysis will not work. Other parts of the App remain available.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the latest version. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
